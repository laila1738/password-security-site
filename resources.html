Get started in 15 minutes

Protect your most important accounts first: email, school, and social media.

Turn on MFA

  1. Open the account's Security settings.
  2. Find "2-Step Verification" or "Two-factor authentication".
  3. Choose an authenticator app if offered, then scan the QR code.
  4. Enter the code to confirm, and save the backup codes somewhere safe.

Checklist

  • Email account has a unique password and MFA.
  • No password is reused across accounts.
  • A password manager holds your passwords.
  • Backup codes are stored offline.
