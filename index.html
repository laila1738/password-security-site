One stolen password should not mean a stolen account.

Most account break-ins start with weak, reused, or phished passwords. This site shows college students how to build strong passwords and add a second layer of protection with multi-factor authentication (MFA).

Objectives

  • Explain what makes a password strong or weak.
  • Recognize common attacks on passwords.
  • Describe how MFA works and compare its common forms.
  • Apply simple habits that protect your own accounts.

Why it matters

Students are targets

School, email, and social accounts hold personal data and grades. Attackers want them too.

Reuse spreads the damage

When one site leaks a password, attackers try it on other sites. Unique passwords stop that chain.

MFA is a safety net

Even if a password is stolen, a second factor can block the login.
